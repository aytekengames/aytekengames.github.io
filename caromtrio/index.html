Mixed reality billiards for Meta Quest 3

Carom Trio

Place a full-size carom table in your living room and play carom and three-cushion billiards with a real cue feel.

SupportPrivacy Policy

Features

  • Your room, your table. Passthrough mixed reality puts the table in your real space. Walk around it and line up shots naturally.
  • Carom and three-cushion. Two classic disciplines with their own rules, scoring and statistics.
  • Single-player career. Climb through tournaments against AI opponents with their own playing styles.
  • Training mode. Free practice to work on spin, angles and cushion shots.
  • Online play. Ranked matches with a skill rating, or a private room to play with a friend.

Platform

Carom Trio is being developed for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S and will be available on the Meta Horizon Store.
